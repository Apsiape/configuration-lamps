\caption{Left: the points of the three rays, a configuration $v$ (filled points), the lamp at $0$, and the generators as moves: $p$ and $q$
move points, and $t$ and $d$ move configurations. Right: the $2^3$ configurations supported on the points $1,2,3$, each carrying a lamp, joined by the
translations $\tau_1,\tau_2,\tau_3$ and folded by the transvection $d=E_{2\leftarrow1}$, which exchanges $e_1$ with $e_1+e_2$ and $e_1+e_3$ with
$e_1+e_2+e_3$ and fixes the other four. $N$ points carry $2^N$ lamps, and any two are brought together by moves that each involve at most
three points.}
\label{fig:lamps}
